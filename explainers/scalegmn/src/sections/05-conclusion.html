5 Takeaways and limitations

This work is a proof of concept: it shows that a symmetry-aware metanetwork can improve the weights of networks it has never seen in a single forward pass, but only on a small scale, with small networks, small model zoos and grayscale CIFAR-10. Every metanetwork we trained also works on a single architecture, the one in its zoo. The natural next step is a metanetwork that handles varying architectures. The graph view of Section 3 makes this possible in principle, since any network can be written as a graph, but it was beyond the data and scope of this paper.
