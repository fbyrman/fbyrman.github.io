5 Conclusion

We asked whether the curvature in current hyperbolic VLMs does the geometric work it is credited with, and found that it does not. The exponential map reduces to the identity early in training, leaving the embedding space effectively Euclidean for the rest of training and at inference. Consistent with this, deleting the map at read-out leaves the scores unchanged, and Euclidean controls trained with the same losses match the hyperbolic models closely. Hyperbolic VLMs have proven effective in practice, and we hope these results encourage researchers to take a critical look at their losses. With the right optimization, we believe they can do much better, and distortion ratios offer a way to measure it.
