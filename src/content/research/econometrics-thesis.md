My MSc thesis in Econometrics and Operations Research at the Vrije Universiteit
Amsterdam, supervised by dr. Agnieszka Borowska. It compares classical econometric methods
(state-space models, Kalman filtering) against Bayesian machine learning (Gaussian process
vector autoregressions) for macroeconomic and financial time-series forecasting.
