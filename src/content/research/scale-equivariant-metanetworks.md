Graph metanetworks take the weights of another neural network as input. Those weights
have symmetries, such as rescalings that leave the function unchanged, and a scale
equivariant metanetwork respects them by construction. This work uses such a metanetwork
for fully amortized optimization.

Extended abstract at the III edition of the Workshop on Unifying Representations in Neural
Models (UniReps).
